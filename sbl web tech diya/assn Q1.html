Shah and Anchor Kutchhi Engineering College

About the College

Shah and Anchor Kutchhi Engineering College was established in 1985 to provide quality technical education. It is managed by Mahavir Education Trust and is affiliated to the University of Mumbai.

The college is an autonomous institute located in Chembur, Mumbai. It offers undergraduate programmes in Computer Engineering, Information Technology, Artificial Intelligence and Data Science, Cyber Security and other engineering fields.
